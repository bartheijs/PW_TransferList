Interaction Modes

  • Click: single click moves item
  • Double click: dbl-click moves item
  • Multi-select: checkboxes + arrow btn
  • Drag & Drop: drag to opposite panel

Persistence

  • Widget does NOT manage state itself
  • onAdd / onRemove actions trigger nanoflows
  • onAddAll / onRemoveAll for bulk moves
  • Commit + refresh datasources in nanoflow
